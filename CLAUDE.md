# Before changing how stories are collected

Read **@docs/COLLECTION.md** before touching discovery, feeds, sitemaps,
scraping, relevance, or the coverage measure. It is the standing record of what
has been measured about real publishers and what has already gone wrong, and it
carries decisions that look arbitrary until you know the failure behind them.

The one thing to carry even if you read nothing else: **the goal is the greatest
number of genuine articles caught within a time window** — recall inside a
window, not article count, not archive depth. A change justified by "it returns
more articles" is not yet justified.
